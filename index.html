How to read Weimar politics

The Weimar Republic used proportional representation, which gave parties seats close to their vote share and allowed many parties into the Reichstag. That made representation broad but coalition-building fragile. This project treats each election as a playable parliament: choose a year, inspect the parties, then test whether a coalition can govern.

Core tension

Republican parties needed compromise; anti-republican parties could win seats while opposing the system itself.

Turning point

After 1930, presidential cabinets and emergency decrees increasingly replaced stable parliamentary government.
