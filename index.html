What this site does

This is a GitHub Pages-ready visual atlas. Pick an election, watch the party balance change, hover over a regional map, build coalitions, study the cabinets, compare long-run charts, and test alternate histories with vote swings and institutional choices.

Proportional politics

Representation was broad, but fractured parliaments made majority cabinets difficult.

Anti-system pressure

Parties could gain seats while rejecting the republic’s legitimacy or democratic norms.

Emergency rule

After 1930, presidential cabinets and decree politics increasingly replaced Reichstag majorities.

Great Depression politics

What did each party propose?

The depression did not just reduce incomes; it shattered coalition discipline. Parties disagreed over austerity, public works, wages, reparations, capitalism, and whether democracy itself should survive.

Project notes

Data and interpretation.

Election data, region notes, party summaries, and government notes are stored directly in app.js. Coalition and alt-history outputs are interpretive teaching tools, not formal historical forecasts.

Suggested sources: German Bundestag historical exhibition on Weimar elections, USHMM’s Weimar Republic and Article 48 entries, and standard compiled Reichstag election tables. Full source notes are in README.md.
